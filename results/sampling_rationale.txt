
SAMPLING RATIONALE — STATEMENT FOR REVIEWERS
=============================================
The synthetic dataset (N=420) replicates a non-random adoption mechanism:
POFC assignment follows a logistic propensity model on budget, duration,
historical CPI and ERP legacy score. The resulting propensity score is
included as a covariate in all model specifications, and inverse-
probability weights (IPW) are applied when training all four models and
when computing hold-out and cross-validated performance metrics. Before
weighting, seven of ten covariates have SMD < 0.20 (budget, duration and
ERP legacy score are imbalanced); after IPW weighting all ten covariates
have SMD < 0.20 (see SMD_IPW in pofc_balance_table.csv). External shocks
affecting 7% of projects and an irreducible noise floor (sigma_eps =
0.065) leave POFC projects with a residual overrun rate of 16.5%. All
results are conditional on this simulated data-generating process and are
not field observations.
